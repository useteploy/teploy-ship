2. TRANSPORT (no ACP)
================================================================================
- Pi does NOT speak ACP (confirmed by package contents: no ACP framing, and
  T3 drives it through a bespoke RPC). Launch: `pi --mode rpc [--no-session]`
  (T3 mcpInjection.ts buildPiRpcLaunch). Framing: LF-delimited JSON, one
  object per line, trailing \r stripped; T3 deliberately avoids readline
  (U+2028/U+2029 would corrupt frames).
- Request shape: {"type":"<command>","id":"<id>",...}. Response shape:
  {"id":"<id>","type":"response","command":"<command>","success":true,
  "data":{...}} or {"success":false,"error":...}. Events arrive as
  non-response records on the same stream.
- Commands T3 uses (adapter.ts): new_session, switch_session (resume),
  prompt (streamingBehavior "steer" for native mid-turn steering),
  abort, get_state, get_available_models, confirm (approvals),
  compact, get_entries.
- A community ACP adapter exists in the registry (entry "pi-acp",
  svkozak/pi-acp, MIT, npx pi-acp@0.0.34) — NOT probed, NOT endorsed for
  Ship; the design note gives Pi a first-class RPC transport in ship-acp.
